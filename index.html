About

A systems mindset, applied to the web

I'm a Computer Systems Technology student at BCIT, currently in Term 2 of the diploma. My focus is full-stack development — how a database schema, an API, and an interface fit together as one system, rather than three separate problems.

BCIT's program is hands-on: every course ends in something built and defended, not just graded. That's shaped how I work — I'd rather ship a smaller feature that's solid than a bigger one held together with guesses.
