About

A systems mindset, applied to the web

I'm a Computer Systems Technology student at BCIT, now in Term 3 on the Data Communications & Internetworking option. I work across the stack, and the networking specialization is teaching me the layer underneath it: what actually happens between the client and the server.

BCIT's program is hands-on: every course ends in something built and defended, not just graded. That's shaped how I work. I'd rather ship a smaller feature that's solid than a bigger one held together with guesses.

A five-page site for a Persian kebab food truck in Vancouver: the truck menu, catering packages, and an order builder customers use before they reach the window.
